# 0031. Regime, seasonality, harmonics, Sen's slope and baseflow of a station

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-13
- Builds on: plan `docs/plans/2026-11-13-station-analyses.md` (decision D11 with D1, D2 and D13, step S3), Nogat's plan `2026-11-12-station-data-and-analyses` (step H2: H12 to H16 and H18, with M12 and M13 of H3; decisions D8, D12, F1), approved by the author on 2026-11-13; ADR-0029 (the reference period, the half-year file), ADR-0028 (the providers' lines)
- Amended by [ADR-0033](0033-review-of-the-station-analyses.md) (2026-11-13): one name per concept, and the variance of Mann–Kendall's S corrected for ties (its points 1 and 5)

## Context

Obserwator's station page shows a river's regime, the season of its floods and low flows, the annual cycle of water temperature, the trends of annual values and the baseflow; its GUI mockup computed them in labelled prototypes (`mockup/gui/server/analyses/_hydro_proto.py`, Sen's slope also in `_meteo_proto.py`, Obserwator branch `feat/gui-mockup`). Nogat's D8 asks for the same number in Obserwator and in Hydrolog, so the methods move into the catalog with their sources. Two of them have no single standard (the plan's risks): Dynowska's (1997) regime types are stated in words, and the Lyne and Hollick filter exists in several variants. The two prototypes also disagree on Sen's intercept and interval. Dynowska's Atlas sheet itself is not available online; Wrzesiński (2017, 2018) quotes her criteria and Perz (2019) applies them to the Kłodzko basin.

## Decision

1. **`stats regime`** (H12, new): a dated daily series and `first_year`, `last_year` (D1). The result `hydrolog-stats-regime/1` has `months` (11 to 10), `monthly_mean` and `parde` (12 each, November to October, as the plan says, where the mockup lists calendar months), `annual_mean`, `regime_type` (`code` 1 to 5 and `criteria`), `spring_share` (the March–April share of the annual volume), `seasonality_index` (Walsh and Lawler, 1981, of the monthly volumes) and the period. Dynowska's types are read with the criteria Wrzesiński (2018, p. 250) quotes; where the sources name no month and no number, Hydrolog keeps the mockup's `REGIME_RULE`: the spring month is the larger of March and April (Perz, 2019, p. 79), the summer month the largest of June to August, "nearly equal" is at least 0.95 of the spring coefficient, the tests run in the order 5, 4, 1 to 3, and "as a rule 130–180 %" of types 4 and 5 is not tested. `regime_type.criteria` gives both months, their coefficients and the limits. A month without a value is `calculation_error` `missing_months`, a mean annual flow that is not positive `non_positive_mean`; the notice `short_reference_period` as D1.
2. **`stats seasonality`** (H13, new): a dated daily series or `half_year_file` (a third `one_of` group), `parameter` `Q`, `H` or `T` (T as D3 of the plan; the mockup offered Q and H), `statistic` `max` or `min`. From a daily series the annual extreme of each complete hydrological year, dated by its first day (a year with a missing day is skipped, notice `incomplete_years`); from the half-year file the larger of the half-year maxima or the smaller of the minima with that half-year's date, the winter one on a tie, as `stats characteristic` takes them (ADR-0029). An extreme without a date is left out with the new notice `undated_extremes` (warning). The dates are angles on their calendar year; the result `hydrolog-stats-seasonality/1` has `years` (`year`, `value`, `date`), `mean_day` (1 = 1 January), `mean_date` (`MM-DD`, where the mockup wrote „8 VI”), `concentration`, `winter_share`, `summer_share` and `source` (Bayliss and Jones, 1993; Burn, 1997). A daily series without a complete year is `calculation_error` `no_complete_years`, a file without a dated extreme `no_dated_extremes`.
3. **`stats harmonics`** (H14, M12, new): `harmonics` 1 to 4 (default 2), least squares on the day of the hydrological year (1 = 1 November) with P = 365.25 d. The result `hydrolog-stats-harmonics/1` has `period` (the mean annual cycle of every day of the period) and `years` (each hydrological year with at least 300 values), each `{mean, harmonics: [{amplitude, phase_day, explained_variance}], r_squared, n_values}`. A year with fewer values is left out with the new notice `short_years_left_out` (info); fewer than 4K + 2 values in the period is `calculation_error` `too_few_values`. The meteorological prototype's period of 365 d changes Słupsk's amplitude of water temperature by 0.0002 °C and the day of its maximum by 0.11 d.
4. **`stats mann-kendall`** (H16, M13, extended): `years` (whole, strictly increasing) and `year_column`; the result gains `sen_slope`, `sen_intercept`, `sen_slope_lower` and `sen_slope_upper` (optional fields, `_ADDED_FIELDS`). Sen's (1968) slope per year with years, otherwise per step of the series (a missing value keeps its step); Conover's (1980) intercept median(x) − b · median(t); Gilbert's (1987, section 16.5) interval with interpolated ranks at 1 − `alpha`, the test's own level, from the test's Var(S); its bounds are `null` when the ranks fall outside 1 to N′ (fewer than 5 values at 0.05). This is `_meteo_proto`'s method, equal to it to the last digit; `_hydro_proto` takes median(x − bt), truncated ranks and a fixed 95 % interval, which differ from these by up to 1.4e-2 (intercept) and 3e-3 to 3.6e-2 (interval at 0.05). With the default `alpha` 0.05 the interval is the plan's 95 % one. In the library, `sen_slope` returns `SenSlope` and takes Gilbert's pairs of one period, and `mann_kendall_test` takes the times and raises for a value that is not finite.
5. **`stats baseflow`** (H18, new): `alpha` in (0, 1) (0.925), `passes` odd and at least 3 (3; an even number is `validation_error` `odd`) and `series_year`. Lyne and Hollick's filter runs on each gapless span of at least 60 days (none is `calculation_error` `no_gapless_span`), reflected by 30 days at each end, in the standard of Ladson et al. (2013) and their published code: each pass starts its quickflow at the first value of its input and keeps it unclipped as the state of the next day, and a baseflow below 0 becomes 0 after the last pass. The mockup's prototype clamps the quickflow to [0, Q] at each step and starts it at 0; only Ladson's version reproduces the published BFI of the Bass River at Loch, 0.1965759 (the prototype gives 0.0944), so Hydrolog's BFI is higher: Kłodzko 0.663 against the prototype's 0.585, Słupsk 0.871 against 0.782. The result `hydrolog-stats-baseflow/1` has `bfi`, `n_values`, `n_spans`, `years` (each year with at least 300 days with a baseflow: `bfi`, `n_values`, `baseflow_mean`, `discharge_mean`; the others in the notice `short_years_left_out`), `recession` (`k`, the median Q(t+1)/Q(t) of falls of at least 5 days, Tallaksen, 1995; `half_life_days`; `n_segments`) and the daily `series_dates` and `series_baseflow` of `series_year`. `series_year` defaults to the last complete year of the period, else the last year with a baseflow, the rule of `cumulative_year` in `stats runoff-volume`; the mockup took the last year with 300 filtered days, the same years on every reproduced series.
6. **Water temperature** (H15) has no command of its own: the days at or above 15, 20, 25 °C and below 1 °C are `stats threshold-events` on T, the yearly maximum with its date `stats characteristic` with `parameter` T, the annual cycle `stats harmonics`.
7. **The providers' lines** (ADR-0028): the four new commands take the option `attribution` (`hydrolog.catalog.groups._attribution.ATTRIBUTION`) and cite it, as `stats mann-kendall` does; an Obserwator export read as `input_file` or `half_year_file` carries its lines (`attribution_source`, `attribution_processed`, `attribution`, `terms_notice`) into the envelope's `attribution` and `terms_notice`.
8. Budgets: the four names join the compact `stats` line, whose clause is unchanged; `AGENTS.md` has 16,320 of 16,384 bytes; every description stays within 600 characters. The catalog has 56 commands in 16 groups; the GUI's „Wszystkie polecenia” shows 52 of them.

## Consequences

- Additive for the existing schemas (`stats mann-kendall` in `_ADDED_FIELDS`); four new result schemas; two new notices with their Polish texts. A caller of the library's `mann_kendall_test` with a `NaN` in the series gets an error.
- The mockup's numbers on Kłodzko and Słupsk are reproduced (the plan's Results), except the BFI and the daily baseflow, which follow Ladson et al. (2013), and Sen's intercept and interval against `_hydro_proto`. Obserwator shows Hydrolog's numbers once it computes them with Hydrolog (Nogat's D8), so its yearly baseflow index rises against the prototype's, by 0.035 to 0.122 at Kłodzko and 0.038 to 0.164 at Słupsk.
- The regime type is Hydrolog's reading of the criteria where the sources are silent (the summer months, 0.95, the order of the tests); `regime_type.criteria` shows what decided it, and the type changes if Dynowska's sheet says otherwise.
- The method of each command, its sources and its checks against published examples are in `docs/COMPUTATION_PATHS.md`, section 3.12.

## Alternatives considered

- **The prototype's filter** (quickflow clamped to [0, Q], started at 0): the mockup's BFI, but it does not reproduce the BFI that Ladson et al. publish with their code, the standard the plan cites.
- **`_hydro_proto`'s intercept and fixed 95 % interval**: one of the mockup's two answers; Conover's intercept is the one the plan cites, and an interval at the test's own level gives one `alpha` for the test and the interval.
- **Monthly lists in calendar order**, as the mockup: every list of months of a hydrological year in Hydrolog runs from November (`stats runoff-volume`, `stats duration-curve`).
- **A command for the water temperature days**: `stats threshold-events` already counts days at or beyond a level, one call per level.
- **A period of 365 days** (the meteorological prototype): 365.25 days, the mean length of a year, is the plan's period; the difference on Słupsk is 0.0002 °C and 0.11 d.
