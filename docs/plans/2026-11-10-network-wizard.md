## Goal

- Build a network in the order a hydrologist thinks of it: the network and its rainfall first, then the elements on the canvas, then each subcatchment's data in the order its method needs them, model first and only the model's fields.
- One way to enter a subcatchment in the whole GUI: Hydrogram zlewni's three steps (Zlewnia, Hietogram, Hydrogram), with the same fields, order, checks, (i) explanations and inputs in Hydrogram zlewni and in Sieć, and one mapping between those inputs and a node of `hydrolog-network/1`.
- Nash by Lutz everywhere: a network document says "Lutz" and the library estimates n and K from the watershed, in a network, in `hydrograph generate` and in the report.
- „Uporządkuj” and the opening of a network without positions use the library's layout as soon as the structure is right, whatever parameters are missing.
- A network that Hydrograf delineates at control points (Change B) opens in Sieć laid out, with the origin of each node, and keeps those origins when Sieć hands the network on.
- Hydrolog still computes every value (GUI plan D8): the browser checks inputs for immediate feedback and draws.

## Decisions

### D1. One component, two hosts: Hydrogram's three steps (A1)

- `hydrogram/steps.js` holds the three steps (out of `data-panel.js`): `SubcatchmentSteps`, driven by props only. Its props are the inputs, `set` (a patch) and `setField`, the errors and warnings by field, what the choice needs, the error above the steps, the values a run gave (`computed`: tc, the CN after AMC, S, Ia, the unit hydrograph's parameters, tp and Qp), the field models, the values of the (i) explanations, PMAXTP's depth, an id prefix (`prefix`), the host's network (`network`: in Sieć the network's rainfall in words and its Δt, which give „Jak w sieci” and the shown Δt, D4), the origins of the inputs (D9) and `onHandoff` (what „Wczytaj z Hydrografa” of step 1 does with the opened hand-off, D10).
- The host keeps the state, the runs and the channels. Hydrogram zlewni keeps its stored inputs, its live `hydrograph generate`, the store's `watershed`, `status` and `context`, and the channel `hydrogram-pmaxtp`. Sieć keeps the node's inputs inside its own (`state.nodes[id]`, a step of the history when a field commits, as the editor's D9 says), runs nothing of its own (it computes on „Oblicz sieć”), gives `computed` from the node's `parameters` in the last `route network` result while the node is unchanged since, the warnings from that node's notices as Hydrogram takes them from its last run (`noticeWarnings`), and its own channel for a PMAXTP depth.
- The checks are Hydrogram's pure functions (`checkInputs`, `requirements`, `panelFields`), so both hosts show, check and need the same fields. A subcatchment whose model is not chosen yet is incomplete in Sieć (a problem of the node in „Problemy sieci”), as Hydrogram zlewni runs nothing before a model is chosen.
- Rejected: a copy of the steps in Sieć (two forms that drift apart, which is what A3 removes); a link to Hydrogram zlewni or a frame (the node would leave Sieć's history, staleness and problems).

### D2. The second left panel: `detail` of ViewLayout (A1)

- ViewLayout gains `detail` (`title`, `sub`, `body`, `foot`, `onClose`): a column right of „Dane”, titled with the node („Zlewnia Z1”), with its own resizer and width (260-560 px, 300 by default, kept like the others). Sieć gives it while the chosen node is a subcatchment made from rainfall: choosing another subcatchment changes its content, choosing another kind of node removes it (a reach, a reservoir, a junction and the outlet keep their settings in „Dane”), and ✕ closes it and keeps the node chosen.
- The centre keeps at least 480 px. When the four columns do not fit, „Wyniki” narrows first, to its least width (340 px), then „Dane” and the panel, evenly, to theirs (260 px); when the centre would still be under 480 px (a layout narrower than 1340 px), „Wyniki” closes while the panel is open, and its button opens it in the panel's place. A 1440 px window leaves the layout 1376 px beside the 64 px rail: „Dane” and the panel take about 278 px each, „Wyniki” 340 px and the centre 480 px.
- On a phone (860 px and below) one left sheet shows „Dane” or the subcatchment's panel, never both: choosing a subcatchment shows its panel with „← Dane”, and „Dane”'s step 3 has „Dane zlewni →” (A1: the panels alternate).
- Focus: choosing a node (a click, the arrow keys on the canvas, the list) opens or changes the panel without moving the focus; Enter on a subcatchment (the editor's key of its parameters, editor D7) moves the focus to the panel's first step. The panel is an `aside` named by its title, as the others are, and its controls meet the editor's D12.

### D3. A node's inputs are Hydrogram's, with one mapping both ways (A3)

- A subcatchment made from rainfall keeps Hydrogram's inputs (`watershed`, `tc_manual`, `tc_method`, `uh_model`, `nash_source`, `clark_kind`, `rain_source`, `pmaxtp_*`, `pmaxtp_fetched`…) as its values in Sieć, where `rain_source` also takes `network` („Jak w sieci”, Sieć's default; D4). `handoff/mapping.js` holds the one mapping: `subcatchmentNode(inputs, place)` makes the node of `hydrolog-network/1` from them through `toOptions`, as a run of `hydrograph generate` takes them, and `subcatchmentInputs(node, origin)` reads a node back. `hydrogramPayload` and `hydrogramInputs` (Hydrogram zlewni's hand-off) are made of the same two functions, so a hand-off of Hydrogram zlewni and a node of Sieć cannot differ.
- Reading takes the library's defaults for what a document leaves out (`uh_model` `scs`, `tc_method` `kirpich`, `amc` `II`, λ 0.2), as `hydrogramInputs` does (`mapping.js:324-329`): the guide's `A`, with `{"model": "curve-number"}`, opens as SCS with Kirpich.
- As in Hydrogram zlewni (2026-11-04: other models' fields are never sent), a watershed field that no step shows for the node's choice stays in the page's inputs, so switching back shows it, and is neither saved nor sent.
- Removed, a clean cut-over: the form of a subcatchment made from rainfall in `node-model.js` (`WATERSHED_MAIN`, `RUNOFF_MAIN`, `UH_FIELDS`, Sieć's copy of `SNYDER_LENGTHS`, the `watershed`, `runoff` and own `rainfall` parts of `buildForms`, `nodeLayout`, `nodeValues`, `nodeOf`), its rendering with „Pozostałe pola zlewni” in `node-form.js`, their texts in `labels/siec.js`, and the tests that typed into them. A subcatchment given its hydrograph („Podany”) or reading its column of the inflows file („Z pliku”) keeps its small form in „Dane”: Hydrogram has no counterpart for a source, and neither has a watershed. The choice among the three stays in „Dane”'s step 3 beside the id, the description and „Odpływa do”.
- Sieć's stored inputs move to `hydrolog-gui/siec/3` with W3 (the network's rainfall) and keep that version through W4, since W3 to W6 merge together; version 2 is not read, as `persist.js` rules.

### D4. The network's rainfall first (A2)

- Step 1 „Sieć” has the name and the description, then „Krok czasowy Δt” (the network's `timestep_min`) and „Opad sieci”: Hydrogram's step 2 (`RainStep`, out of `hydrogram/data-panel.js` into `hydrogram/rain-step.js` with W3) bound to the network. „Brak” (a network of given hydrographs needs none), PMAXTP (the point, p, the duration, POT or AMP; the depth fetched by `precip pmaxtp` on the channel `siec-pmaxtp` and kept with the inputs as `pmaxtp_fetched`, as in Hydrogram zlewni), a design hyetograph, or a series at Δt; then the session's files, as today. The document's top `rainfall` is what a run of `hydrograph generate` takes: `design` with the fetched depth as `total_mm`, or `series`. The fetch is a hook, `usePmaxtp`, that both sections use.
- „Nowa sieć” opens step 1 there, and the empty canvas says to give the network's rainfall first and then to place the elements. Nothing is locked: a file, a hand-off or a network of given hydrographs may come without it, and the editor's keys and single-pointer ways stay as they are. A subcatchment made from rainfall that has neither its own rainfall nor the network's is a problem of its node („Brak opadu: ustaw opad sieci w kroku 1 albo własny opad zlewni”), shown before „Oblicz sieć” (the library's `missing_field` `rainfall`).
- In the subcatchment's panel, step 2 „Hietogram” starts at „Jak w sieci” (`rain_source` `network`): the network's rainfall in words, read-only, with a link to step 1. „Własny opad” is the exception and shows Hydrogram zlewni's fields of step 2 for the node's own `rainfall`. Δt is the network's in both places: shown in the panel, not edited there; a series at another step is `timestep_mismatch`.
- The top rainfall of a hand-off, with the PMAXTP record of its `provenance.rainfall` (the point, p, the duration, the method), becomes the network's rainfall in step 1 the way Hydrogram zlewni reads a hand-off's rainfall: the depth remembered without a fetch when the record has the point (`rainfallInputs`, from `hydrogramInputs`). „Dodaj zlewnię z Hydrografa” keeps its rule: a rainfall that differs from the network's becomes the node's own.

### D5. Nash by Lutz in the document: `nash_estimation` (A5)

- One new option of `hydrograph generate`, and so of `report generate`, which takes the same options, and one field of the `curve-number` runoff of `hydrolog-network/1`: `nash_estimation`, a string with the one value `lutz`, optional, without a default, after `nash_k_min` in the options and in the canonical runoff. Its description: how Nash's n and K are estimated from the watershed instead of being given; `lutz` (Lutz 1984; KZGW 2017), from the fields below. Polish: „Estymacja n i K”.
- The inputs are watershed fields, as those of `tc_method` are (ADR-0019): L `channel_length_km`, Lc `Lc_km`, J `channel_slope_m_per_m`, n_M `manning_n`, and U `urban_pct` and W `forest_pct` when given, else 0 (as `uh nash`); `area_km2` scales the unit hydrograph. These are the fields that Hydrogram zlewni and Kalkulatory map today, so no number changes. One table beside `TC_INPUTS` in `hydrolog._ranges` (`NASH_INPUTS`) is read by `WatershedParameters.nash_parameters("lutz")`, the arguments of `NashIUH.from_lutz`, which both the runoff model and `compute_hydrograph` call: one path for the network, the command and the report.
- Rules, with the reasons of `tc_method`: only with `uh_model` `nash` (`not_applicable`); not with `nash_n` or `nash_k_min` (`conflict`); Nash needs n and K or `nash_estimation` (`required` on `nash_n`, whose hint names `nash_estimation`); another value is `choice`; a missing input is `missing_field` with `details.option` the first, `details.missing` all and `details.method` `lutz` (in a network also `details.node_id`); Lc greater than L is `maximum` on `Lc_km`. `hydrograph generate` given `area_km2` and `cn` instead of a watershed has nothing to estimate from: `not_applicable`, as `tc_method` there („computes tc from a watershed, but none was given”).
- The result of `hydrograph generate` gains `nash_estimation` (as it has `tc_method`), and its `uh_parameters` carry the estimated `n` and `k_min`. `describe()` of the node lists `nash_estimation` (`given`) and `nash_n` and `nash_k_min` (`computed`), so the node's parameters in `route network` and in the schematic show them. `report generate` prints Lutz's steps from its existing section. `hydrograph generate` serves Lutz's area range in `typical_ranges` (the warning `outside_typical_range`, method `lutz`); estimated n and K are not checked against the ranges of given ones (the library's rule for estimates), so PROGRESS's open question on those ranges no longer concerns Lutz.
- ADR-0024 records it and amends ADR-0012 (a field of the runoff). The version stays `/1`: the field is optional, and the document is unreleased anyway (AGI-4.1). An older Hydrolog refuses a document that has it (`unknown_field`), as it refused ADR-0012's and ADR-0023's fields; Retman reads it once its release set takes W1, and Hydrograf may write it but need not. `hydrolog.testing.curve_number_runoff` takes it already (`**options`, checked by the real reader); its docstring names it. Urban regression is not a value of it: its inputs, the effective rainfall and its duration, belong to the storm, not to the watershed.

### D6. Hydrogram zlewni takes the same path: one run (the choice A5 leaves open)

- Nash by Lutz sends `nash_estimation: "lutz"` with the watershed to `hydrograph generate` and no longer runs `uh nash` first. One run: the CLI line it exports says `--nash-estimation lutz` and reproduces the result in one command (today the line carries n and K that another run estimated, without saying so); the hand-off carries `nash_estimation` instead of numbers, so whoever opens it estimates again from the watershed it has; Hydrogram zlewni, Sieć and the CLI go through one function of the library. The n and K shown read-only in Lutz's block come from the result's `uh_parameters`.
- Not through `route network` of a one-node document: the section shows `hydrograph generate`'s result (P, Pe, the balance, the unit hydrograph's ordinates), and the node and the command give the same hydrograph (W1 tests it).
- Removed: `LUTZ_COMMAND`, `lutzOptions`, `LUTZ_SOURCES`, the merging of `uh nash`'s notices, the fetch of `uh nash`'s document for its range (now in `hydrograph generate`'s), `LUTZ_ORIGIN` in the hand-off's provenance, and the chip of Lutz's lag time (the run gives n, K and the unit hydrograph's tp and Qp, which stay; Kalkulatory's `uh nash` still gives the lag).
- A hand-off written before W1, with n and K and `provenance.runoff` of `uh nash`, opens with its n and K given: the same numbers, since they were Lutz's estimates for the same watershed.
- The GUI plan's D13 sentence on Nash by Lutz is superseded; W1 adds the erratum.

### D7. The structure alone: `route schematic --structure-only` (A4)

- A boolean option `structure_only` of `route schematic` (default false). With it the command reads of the document only `schema`, `name`, `timestep_min` (when it is a positive number), each node's `id`, `type` and `from`, and each headwater subcatchment's `watershed.area_km2` (when every headwater has a positive one, the rule by which ADR-0013 weighs the branches). It checks the structure with ADR-0010's reasons and nothing else; any other field may be missing or wrong. It ignores positions (the layout is always computed) and refuses `inflows_file` and `time_column` (`conflict`: no file is read). Python: `hydrolog.routing.structure_schematic(document, *, main_stem=None)`; the `main_stem` option works as without the flag.
- The result is `hydrolog-network-schematic/1` with `structure_only: true` (at the top, after `layout`), `layout` `computed`, `has_result` false, every node's `parameters` `[]`, no node `description`, and `timestep_min` only when the document gives it. In the JSON Schema `timestep_min` leaves `required` and `structure_only` is added; every schematic of the existing modes is unchanged, byte for byte. `/1` stays: the schematic is unreleased (AGI-4.1) and nothing outside Hydrolog reads it.

### D8. Sieć lays out the structure (A4 in the page)

### D9. Network hand-offs and the origin of each node (A6)

- A hand-off whose payload holds more than one subcatchment opens in Sieć, as `targetOf` already decides, as a ready schematic: at the payload's positions, else at the structure's layout (D8), with the network's rainfall in step 1 (D4), each subcatchment's values through `subcatchmentInputs` (D3) and its other nodes as they come. - Writing (decided with A6 on 2026-11-10 by delegated authority, beyond Nogat's D8, which leaves it out of Nogat's plan; so that the origins survive Hydrograf → Hydrolog → file → Hydrolog): Sieć's hand-off keeps `provenance` (the received one, or the file's) and writes `node_provenance` under the nodes' current ids. The network's own rainfall in `provenance.rainfall` follows Hydrogram's rule: the received record while unchanged, else the PMAXTP record of its fetch, else `{"source": "user"}`.

### D10. „Wczytaj z Hydrografa” in the subcatchment's panel (A1)

- In step 1 of the panel it reads a hand-off of one watershed (Hydrograf's `delineate-watershed` or `generate-hydrograph`, or Hydrogram zlewni's) into the chosen node: the watershed, the runoff and the rainfall as Hydrogram zlewni reads them (`subcatchmentInputs`), the rainfall as „Jak w sieci” when it equals the network's, else as the node's own. One step of the history.
- A hand-off of a network is refused there, with the two ways to take it: „Wczytaj z Hydrografa” of step 1 (it replaces the network) or „Dodaj zlewnię z Hydrografa”.
