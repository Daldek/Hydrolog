- **`hydrolog-testing` in the Nogat registry**: `hydrolog.testing` exists ([ADR-0011](decisions/0011-public-testing-module.md)); Nogat adds `hydrolog-testing` to the `exchange` of the Hydrolog edges that carry files (Hydraulik reads the `route network` tables and the `precip hietogram` envelope). Done by Nogat, not here. Hydraulik pins Hydrolog v0.7.0, which has no `hydrolog.testing`: its tests that use it need the pin to move to the routing release (see Hydraulik's PROGRESS).
- **Producers' names**: Hydrolog's documents expect Kartograf `soil download --output-dir` (with `files[].path`, `download_soil(output_dir=...)`) and Obserwator `series export --output`; confirm them when those releases land.
- **Control points: areas and channels** ([ADR-0012](decisions/0012-network-objects-rainfall-and-runoff.md)): `ControlPointNetwork` takes the residual area and the channel of every point from the application and checks neither that the areas add up to the catchment nor that a channel matches the stream (Hydrolog does not know areas of a subcatchment that is given its hydrograph). Decide with Hydrograf's first use whether an optional check of the sum of areas of the made subcatchments is wanted; a document of the points of its own was left out until a consumer needs one.
- **Continuous runoff models and areal rainfall**: a `RunoffModel` that keeps a state (GR4J, HBV) and a `RainfallSource` of areal rainfall (Obserwator, radar, PMAXTP) are classes that implement the protocols and a registry entry; the first needs a decision on the longer time axis and the warm-up, the second a `request` issue in the repository that owns the data (DEV-17.5).
- **Nash's typical n and K** (`hydrolog._ranges`): the given n 1–10 and K 10–200 min have no published source; the author kept them on 2026-11-04 as to be verified (`verified` false, „Hydrolog; no published source, to be verified”, notice `outside_unverified_range`, „do weryfikacji” in the GUI). Open: a published source that confirms or replaces them, after which they become verified ranges with `outside_typical_range`. Hydrogram zlewni passes the n and K that Lutz's `uh nash` estimates to `hydrograph generate` as given values, so there these ranges check Lutz's estimates too.
